DOMAIN GUIDE

Make every domain serve the mission.

Work left to right through each card at the start, revisit MONITOR items after significant events or every two hours, and use COMMIT items before irreversible action. RECOVER items protect the next decision cycle.

C2

Command, Intent & ROE

Keep activity aligned to purpose and authority

KEY DECISIONDoes the situation require reinforcement, restraint, engagement, or disengagement?
GREEN WHEN

Actions support intent, authorities are clear, and a reserve remains.

HOLD WHEN

Purpose, authority, target identity, or escalation state is unclear.

ISR

Intelligence & Contact Picture

Turn intermittent detections into useful decisions

KEY DECISIONWhich uncertainty must BLUE reduce next—and which can it safely tolerate?
GREEN WHEN

Priority axes have overlapping coverage and uncertainty is explicit.

HOLD WHEN

A stale or single-source track is driving irreversible action.

AIR

Air Operations

Generate useful sorties without consuming tomorrow

KEY DECISIONHow much airpower should BLUE commit now while preserving the next cycle?
GREEN WHEN

Coverage is continuous, enablers are protected, and a ready reserve exists.

HOLD WHEN

The package cannot recover safely or leaves no answer to a counterattack.

SUW

Surface Maneuver

Create sea room, mutual support, and favorable geometry

KEY DECISIONShould the surface force close, hold, reposition, or open distance?
GREEN WHEN

Groups retain maneuver room, layered defense, and more than one exit route.

HOLD WHEN

Closing compresses reaction time or places multiple high-value units in one threat basket.

USW

Undersea Warfare

Protect the force without wasting time or revealing hunters

KEY DECISIONDoes this datum justify prosecution, avoidance, screening, or continued search?
GREEN WHEN

High-value approaches are screened and uncertain contacts are contained.

HOLD WHEN

Prosecution empties the screen, exhausts aviation, or risks fratricide.

AMD

Air & Missile Defense

Defend priorities through layers, not wishful coverage

KEY DECISIONWhich asset must survive this raid, and which engagements best ensure it?
GREEN WHEN

Priority units have layered coverage and capacity for another raid.

HOLD WHEN

A new commitment strips the defended asset or consumes the final defensive layer.

PAUSE BEFORE COMMITMENTFive questions before an irreversible action
  1. What effect am I trying to create?
  2. Is the contact current and correctly identified?
  3. Am I authorized to act here and now?
  4. Are timing, support, and recovery aligned?
  5. What combat power must remain afterward?
FX

Fires & Strike

Create the required effect with the smallest sound commitment

KEY DECISIONIs striking now more valuable than preserving the option to strike later?
GREEN WHEN

The effect advances the mission and BLUE retains defensive and follow-on capacity.

HOLD WHEN

Authority, ID, recovery, proportionality, or post-strike survival is doubtful.

EW

Electromagnetic Warfare

Trade signature for information only when it pays

KEY DECISIONShould this force radiate, remain passive, deceive, or jam right now?
GREEN WHEN

BLUE has the needed picture without presenting a stable targeting solution.

HOLD WHEN

Emissions are habitual, unsupported, or reveal high-value geometry for little gain.

LIT

Littoral & Land-Based Support

Keep bases and coastal systems functionally useful

KEY DECISIONWhich base or coastal function is essential to the next six hours?
GREEN WHEN

At least two viable operating locations support recovery and sortie generation.

HOLD WHEN

A launch or emission exposes a scarce fixed function without decisive benefit.

LOG

Sustainment & Force Generation

Make combat power available at the decision point

KEY DECISIONWhat resource will become the binding constraint if BLUE continues this tempo?
GREEN WHEN

The main effort is supplied and the force can absorb one more demand spike.

HOLD WHEN

The action consumes minimum defensive reserves or creates unrecoverable aircraft.

PRT

Protection, Recovery & CSAR

Preserve people, platforms, and tomorrow’s options

KEY DECISIONCan BLUE recover the force or survivor without creating a larger loss?
GREEN WHEN

Recovery capacity exists and damaged units are moving toward safety.

HOLD WHEN

The rescue lacks threat suppression, reliable datum, or a survivable exit.

AAR

Assessment & Day Handoff

Know what changed and preserve it for tomorrow

KEY DECISIONAt 2400Z, should BLUE continue, disengage, reposition, or request escalation?
GREEN WHEN

The save is preserved, state is accounted for, and Day 2’s first decisions are clear.

HOLD WHEN

Aircraft remain unrecovered, losses are ambiguous, or finalization has not run.
